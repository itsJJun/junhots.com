01

Live talents

Every talent slot sits right above the game's own HUD and fills in as you pick. The talent name slides in over the header, and the next slot glows softly in your rank's color.

  • All 7 levels with the key under each icon, 10 in gold and 20 in coral
  • No box: a soft dark fade keeps it readable without covering the game
  • Size slider and darkness slider, lined up with the game HUD by default
  • Your hero is found by itself when the match loads, a pick is one hotkey
02

Rank, score and streaks

Your rank badge, your score this session, win rate and the last 10 games as dots. A win streak warms the panel up, a losing streak turns it cold, and every result gets its own Victory or Defeat moment.

  • Sharp Storm League badge, in the header, on the corner, on the left edge or off
  • Win dots green, losses red, a losing streak icy blue
  • Results count by themselves, a wrong one can be undone

Shows at the start of a match, then gets out of the way.

03

Match-start banner

When the match loads, a tab under each health bar shows that hero's tier and win rate, and the box under the score shows who has the better draft.

  • Uses the map's own tier list when the map has one
  • Draft edge counts tiers, win rates, counters, synergy and team comp, the same math as the Draft helper
  • Power combos like Auriel + Cho'gall get a gold link and play at their combo tier
04

Tier list on stream

When chat types !tierlist between games, my tier list pops up on stream for a few seconds. Viewers can ask for a map or a role.

  • !tierlist ds support shows Dragon Shire supports, in any order
  • Power combos listed along the bottom, with the tier each pair plays at
  • Stays off screen while I'm in a game, chat is told to ask again after
  • Or show it yourself from the control panel for as long as you like

05

Chat commands

The overlay answers in Twitch chat with your own wording. Each command has its own on/off switch.

!tierlist
Tier list in chat and on stream, by map and role
!rank
Your rank, Master and GM number too. "what rank?" works
!builds
Points to every hero's builds on junhots.com
!patch
What the latest HotS patch changed
06

Tier list editor

Drag heroes into tiers, per map if you like. One Save updates the overlay, the chat replies and junhots.com.

  • Counters, synergy, style tags and power combos for the draft edge
  • Paste win rates straight from Heroes Profile
  • Save as image for Discord or socials
  • After each ranked game, the result goes to my match history
